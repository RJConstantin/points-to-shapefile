Point Data Utility

Points to Shapefile

Turn an Excel table, CSV or copied and pasted point list into a point shapefile. Choose the coordinate columns, define the coordinate system, select the attributes to keep and name the output file.

Files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to a server.

1. Add point data

Use an Excel or CSV file or paste rows copied directly from Excel or another table.

Which coordinate system should I select?

Use the projection identified by the source whenever possible. If you are not sure, use the location and coordinate pattern below as a guide.

If your coordinates / points are here and look like this... Select this coordinate system
Longitude and latitude in degrees. Alberta values are usually X about -120 to -110 and Y about 49 to 60. Use the datum stated by the source. WGS 84 latitude / longitude or NAD 1983 latitude / longitude
Western Alberta, west of about 114°W, with metre coordinates such as X 300000 to 700000 and Y 5400000 to 6700000. NAD 1983 UTM Zone 11N
Eastern Alberta, east of about 114°W, with metre coordinates such as X 300000 to 700000 and Y 5400000 to 6700000. Fort McMurray and Fort McKay are in this zone. NAD 1983 UTM Zone 12N
From an Alberta government, forestry or resource workflow specifically identified as 10TM or AEP Forest. The values commonly look like six-digit X and seven-digit Y coordinates, similar to UTM. NAD 1983 10TM AEP Forest (EPSG 3400)
From an Alberta 3TM dataset. Eastings are generally much smaller than UTM or 10TM and may be near zero or negative. Choose the central meridian that covers the site. 3TM 117°W for western Alberta, 3TM 114°W for central Alberta, or 3TM 111°W for eastern Alberta

Important: coordinates such as X 473566 and Y 6362909 could be either UTM or 10TM. If the wrong one is selected, the points can still appear in Alberta but be hundreds of kilometres from the correct location.

2. Set the coordinate fields

Select which columns contain X and Y. For projected systems, X is Easting and Y is Northing. For latitude / longitude, X is Longitude and Y is Latitude.

Fields to keep

All fields are kept by default. Shapefile attribute names are limited to 10 characters so longer names will be shortened in the DBF.

3. Create the shapefile

The download is a ZIP containing the shapefile components, including the projection file.
